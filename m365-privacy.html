Privacy Policy — LinkSheet for Microsoft 365

Last updated: September 10, 2026 · CropSettle LLC

LinkSheet for Microsoft 365 ("the add-in") is an Excel add-in published by CropSettle LLC. This policy explains what the add-in accesses and what it does — and does not — do with your data. It covers this add-in only; our desktop applications and our Google Sheets add-on have their own policies.

What the add-in accesses

  • The workbook you have open — to read cell values, so it can tell which cell names which file, and to write the hyperlinks you ask for. Link Files reads and writes the active worksheet; Repair Broken Links and Clear Hyperlinks read and write every sheet in the open workbook. This happens through Excel's own add-in interface and needs no Microsoft account permission at all.
  • Your name — shown at the top of the panel so you can see which account is signed in. Microsoft Graph permission: User.Read.
  • File names and links in the OneDrive folder you choose — to match files to your cells and to build the hyperlinks. Permission: Files.Read, which is read-only. The add-in reads each file's name and its link. It does not open, download, or read the contents of any file, and it never creates, changes, moves or deletes anything in your OneDrive. A shared folder that you have added to your own OneDrive ("Add shortcut to My files") is reached through this same permission.

Those two — User.Read and Files.Read — are the only Microsoft permissions the add-in requests. Microsoft shows them to you the first time the add-in asks for them. On Excel for the web the sign-in is usually silent, because Excel itself vouches for the account you are already using; in that case you can review the permissions at any time at myapps.microsoft.com (work or school accounts) or account.microsoft.com (personal accounts).

What the add-in does NOT do

  • It does not copy, store, or transmit your workbook, your files, or their contents to CropSettle LLC or to any third party. Your workbook is never uploaded to us. We operate no server that receives your content, because there is nothing for one to do.
  • It does not read file contents — only names and links.
  • It does not look outside the folder you choose for a run.
  • It does not use analytics, telemetry, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not sell or share personal data, and it does not use your content to train models.

Where processing happens

Everything happens on your own device, in the browser or Office client running the add-in. The add-in talks directly to Microsoft — to login.microsoftonline.com to sign you in and to graph.microsoft.com to list the folder you picked. Nothing is routed through CropSettle LLC.

What is stored, and where

The add-in keeps three things, all in your browser's local storage on your own device, and none of them ever leaves it:

  • The sign-in token issued by Microsoft, kept by Microsoft's own authentication library so you are not asked to sign in on every run. Signing out removes it. We never see it.
  • A licence key, if you have entered one. The key is a signed string that the add-in checks on your device; nothing about it is sent anywhere, and there is no licence server. "Remove key" deletes it.
  • A count of Repair runs used on the plan without a key — a single number, so the add-in knows how many remain. It is not sent anywhere and it records nothing about the workbook, the folder, or the links.

No file names, cell values, folder names, results, or other usage records are retained after a run finishes. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all three items above.

Undo and safety

The add-in does not make separate backup copies. Excel's own version history — File ▸ Info ▸ Version History in Excel on the web, or the equivalent in your Office client — is the safety net, and any run can be rolled back there.

Children

The add-in is a business productivity tool. It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect information from them — or, in fact, from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the date above. Because the add-in sends nothing to us, material changes are unlikely.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about users of this add-in — everything listed above stays on your device — so there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on request. If you believe otherwise, write to us and we will look into it.
